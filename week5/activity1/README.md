Design of the College Management System is represented by 3 diagrams.

### Use Case Diagram

The Use Case diagram displays the relationship between students and lecturers with regard to the College Management System. The students have access to view the course details and assignments, enroll in courses, submit assignments, and view their marks. On the other hand, the lecturers view and update their allocated courses, handle lectures and assignments, view enrolled students and record and publish marks. Generally, this Use Case diagram illustrates all the services provided by each type of user in the system.



### Class Diagram

The class diagram illustrates the various components of the College Management System and how these components interact. Students and lecturers are both users in this college management system. Lecturers handle courses, lectures, assignments, and marks. The students on the other hand enroll for courses and submit assignments. These submissions might be marked and later published.



### Activity Diagram

The activity diagram depicts how the process of assigning and grading occurs in a linear way. A lecturer generates and makes an assignment visible, which is viewed and submitted by the students. Validation checks whether the assignment and submission are valid. Once submitted, the work is reviewed by the lecturer, and a score along with feedback is recorded and made visible or saved as a draft for future use.
